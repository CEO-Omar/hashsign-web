Messaging

Encrypted 1:1 messaging

Direct messages are encrypted on your device before they are stored. HashSign stores ciphertext, not the private key or the message text. Resetting a device key publishes a new public key.

United States only

Biometric Face Signature

Face Signature is US-only. A SHA-384 commitment bound to the document can be anchored on Hedera. Anchoring is a paid feature. Raw frames are wiped on the device. An invited signer in the United States can complete a required face signature.

United States only

US Government ID Verification

A driver's license, state ID, or passport is read on the device. HashSign keeps a redacted attestation, not the ID photo or the ID number. US ID verification is US-only.

Seal

SHA-384 on Hedera

The finished PDF is hashed on your device. That digest, not the contract, is what gets anchored.

Post-quantum

ML-DSA signature

An ML-DSA signature covers the document manifest. The SHA-384 anchor covers the finished file.

Mandate

Electronic Agent Mandate

An authorized agent records the Electronic Agent Mandate on the activity log and the sealed file. A SHA-384 digest shows the file has not changed since it was sealed. It does not prove who signed it.
