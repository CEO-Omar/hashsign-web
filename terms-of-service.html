4. Messages and documents

From HashSign version 1.3.0, 1:1 direct messages are encrypted on your device before they are stored. Private keys stay in Apple iCloud Keychain or the Android Keystore. HashSign's servers do not have those private keys or the message plaintext.

Deal Room messages and Vault documents are protected by TLS in transit and encryption at rest. That protection is separate from 1:1 encrypted messaging. A sealed document can carry an on-device post-quantum signature and a SHA-384 digest anchored to the Hedera Consensus Service. Only the digest is anchored, not the document. Anchoring is a paid feature. HashSign cannot delete a digest that has already been written to the ledger.

Messages sent before version 1.3.0 were not encrypted on the device before they were stored.
